# 范本库

## 怎样用

这里列的是经得起时间、值得反复学的写作方法书和作品。只读和当前任务相关的那一节。

- **借手法，不借句子。** 看它怎么开头，怎么挑细节，怎么安排顺序，怎么控制距离，然后用在自己的材料上。
- **不借经历和身份。** 作者的经历、实测结果、个人判断属于作者本人，不能挪成用户的经历或当前产品的事实。
- **成稿里不必点名。** 除非用户问为什么这样写，或者文体本身需要引用。
- **正式引用要核原文。** 这里的描述是概括，不是逐字引文；引号里的短句都是常见的公开引文，正式使用前仍应对照原书或原文。
- 带链接的条目，是之前查实过的公开页面；没有链接的，按书名、篇名查找。

## 写作方法书

### 中文

- **胡适《文学改良刍议》（1917）**：“须言之有物”“务去滥调套语”，白话文的起点，也是反套话的起点。
- **鲁迅《答北斗杂志社问》**：删去可有可无的字、句、段；不生造谁也不懂的形容词。
- **刘勰《文心雕龙·镕裁》**：“规范本体谓之镕，翦截浮词谓之裁”，一千五百年前讲的就是先定中心、再删浮词。[维基文库](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/文心雕龍/鎔裁)
- **夏丏尊、叶圣陶《文心》（1934）**：用一个中学生的故事讲写作和阅读，平易近人。
- **叶圣陶的写作论述**：提倡“写话”，写下来的要念得出、像人话。
- **吕叔湘、朱德熙《语法修辞讲话》（1951）**：用词、造句、标点上的毛病，病例丰富。
- **朱光潜《谈文学》**，尤其《咬文嚼字》一篇：斟酌字句就是斟酌思想。
- **余光中《怎样改进英式中文》、思果《翻译研究》**：欧化中文的病例和改法。
- **刘军强《写作是门手艺》**：写给中文研究者的写作书，讲段落、修改和“一段一点”。
- **张大春《文章自在》**：借古人的文章讲意旨与材料，练习要先有目的。[出版社试读](https://www.cite.com.tw/book?id=67547)
- **《中文文案排版指北》**：中英混排、空格、标点的实用规范。[GitHub](https://github.com/sparanoid/chinese-copywriting-guidelines)

### English

- **George Orwell, "Politics and the English Language" (1946)**: stale language as a substitute for thought; six rules, the last of which tells you to break the others rather than write something barbarous.
- **William Zinsser, *On Writing Well***: clutter, simplicity, the writer as a person on the page.
- **Strunk and White, *The Elements of Style***: "Omit needless words."
- **Joseph M. Williams, *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace***: characters as subjects, actions as verbs, old before new, cohesion and coherence. The most useful sentence-level book there is.
- **George Gopen and Judith Swan, "The Science of Scientific Writing" (*American Scientist*, 1990)**: topic position and stress position; readers expect new information at the end of the sentence.
- **Steven Pinker, *The Sense of Style* (2014)**: classic style; the curse of knowledge. Pinker's adapted essay "The Source of Bad Writing" (*Wall Street Journal*, 2014) is a short way in.
- **Francis-Noël Thomas and Mark Turner, *Clear and Simple as the Truth***: the original account of classic style.
- **Thomas S. Kane, *The Oxford Essential Guide to Writing***: purpose shapes every choice; how paragraphs develop; description and narration.
- **Anne Lamott, *Bird by Bird***: shitty first drafts, short assignments, permission to write badly first.
- **Ursula K. Le Guin, *Steering the Craft***: sentence sound, point of view, and narrative voice, with exercises.
- **George Saunders, *A Swim in a Pond in the Rain* (2021)**: reading Russian short stories line by line to see how they hold attention; revision as noticing where your own attention drops.
- **John McPhee, *Draft No. 4* (2017)**: structure, drafting, and the long patience of nonfiction.
- **Helen Sword, *Stylish Academic Writing* (2012)** and her *New York Times* piece "Zombie Nouns" (2012). [Author page](https://www.helensword.com/stylish-academic-writing)
- **Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle***: answer first; SCQA.
- **Booth, Colomb and Williams, *The Craft of Research***: claims, reasons, evidence, warrants.
- **Graff and Birkenstein, *They Say / I Say***: entering a conversation; templates for argument.
- **Ann Handley, *Everybody Writes* (2014)**: writing for content and marketing. [Public excerpt](https://copyblogger.com/simple-not-simplistic/)
- **Chip Heath and Dan Heath, *Made to Stick* (2007)**: why some ideas are remembered: simple, unexpected, concrete, credible, emotional, stories.
- **Elmore Leonard, "Easy on the Adverbs, Exclamation Points and Especially Hooptedoodle" (*New York Times*, 2001)**: "If it sounds like writing, I rewrite it."

## 按体裁

### 口播、视频与演讲

- **3Blue1Brown（Grant Sanderson）**：旁白和动画一步一步对齐，先直觉后符号。[Large Language Models explained briefly](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/mini-llm/)、[Attention in transformers](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/attention/)
- **Jeff Su**：效率工具教程，动作之后立刻说后果，再说为什么这样选。例如 *Learn 80% of NotebookLM in Under 13 Minutes!*（YouTube，2024）。
- **Marques Brownlee（MKBHD）**：评测结构清楚，判断明确，最后说适合谁。
- **Veritasium（Derek Muller）**：用误解开场，再纠正。
- **罗翔**：法律讲成故事，复杂概念落到具体的人。
- **李永乐**：白板上一步一步推。
- **Steve Jobs，2005 年斯坦福毕业演讲**：“三个故事”的简单结构；2007 年 iPhone 发布会：重复与揭示。
- **Chris Anderson, *TED Talks: The Official TED Guide to Public Speaking* (2016)**：throughline。
- **Nancy Duarte, *Resonate* (2010)**：听众是主角。[第一章](https://www.resonatebook.com/chapter-1/)
- **Michael Alley, *The Craft of Scientific Presentations***：assertion-evidence 幻灯片。

### 解释、教程与技术写作

- **Diátaxis**：教程、操作指南、参考、解释四分法。[diataxis.fr](https://diataxis.fr/)
- **Julia Evans**：好奇、诚实地把难题拆成可检查的小问题。[How do you tell if a problem is caused by DNS?](https://jvns.ca/blog/2021/11/04/how-do-you-tell-if-a-problem-is-caused-by-dns/)
- **Simon Willison**：先校准期待，再讲具体做法和失败时怎么接手。[Here's how I use LLMs to help me write code](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Mar/11/using-llms-for-code/)
- **Bartosz Ciechanowski**：交互式长文，一层一层搭起复杂系统。[ciechanow.ski](https://ciechanow.ski/)
- **Richard Feynman**：从现象出发，承认未知。
- **Randall Munroe, *Thing Explainer* (2015)**：只用最常见的一千个英文词解释复杂的东西。
- **阮一峰**：先说结论，例子短而准。[网络日志](https://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/)；技术测评可参考 [豆包 AI 编程简单测评](https://www.ruanyifeng.com/blog/2025/06/doubao-ai-coding.html)（用一个产物贯穿全文，观察逐步改变判断）。
- **网道（WangDoc）**：中文技术教程。[JavaScript 教程·定时器](https://wangdoc.com/javascript/async/timer)
- **卢昌海**：严谨又好读的中文物理、数学科普。

### 产品、品牌与广告

- **David Ogilvy, *Confessions of an Advertising Man* (1963), *Ogilvy on Advertising* (1983)**：具体性、研究、尊重读者。劳斯莱斯“电子钟”标题。
- **DDB 为大众甲壳虫做的 “Think Small”、为 Avis 做的 “We Try Harder”**：立场型广告的经典。
- **Patagonia, “Don't Buy This Jacket” (2011)**：反直觉但真诚的品牌立场。[Patagonia 原文](https://www.patagonia.com/stories/planet/activism/dont-buy-this-jacket-black-friday-and-the-new-york-times/story-18615.html)
- **April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome* (2019)**：定位五问。
- **Apple Newsroom**：功能接到使用场景，条件放脚注。[AirTag 发布稿](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2021/04/apple-introduces-airtag/)
- **Linear changelog**：更新日志从用户能做什么写起，配图，少形容词。
- **农夫山泉、小米早期的广告语**：一个画面、一个立场。
- **Mailchimp Content Style Guide**：voice 和 tone 的区分。[styleguide.mailchimp.com](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/)

### 商业与管理

- **Jeff Bezos 历年致股东信**：清楚、有立场、用例子讲原则。[2016 年致股东信](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2016-letter-to-shareholders)（Day 1、disagree and commit）
- **Ryan Singer, *Shape Up* (2019)**：pitch 怎么写，问题、投入上限、方案和不做的东西放在一起。[Write the Pitch](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/1.5-chapter-06)
- **Patrick McKenzie, *Bits about Money***：把复杂的金融机制写成普通人能懂的长文。
- **Matt Levine, *Money Stuff***：专业又好笑。

### 工作沟通

- **Guido van Rossum, “Transfer of power” (2018)**：Python 创始人宣布卸任 BDFL 的公开邮件，清楚区分确定的变化和尚未决定的安排。[邮件原文](https://mail.python.org/pipermail/python-committers/2018-July/005664.html)
- **Cal Newport, “Deep Habits: Write Your Own E-mail Protocols”**：回复里直接推进事情，而不是来回问“什么时候方便”。[原文](https://calnewport.com/deep-habits-write-your-own-e-mail-protocols/)
- **阮一峰在开源项目里的回复**：用一句技术理由回应问题，不绕弯。[例子](https://github.com/ruanyf/es6tutorial/issues/647#issuecomment-383814006)
