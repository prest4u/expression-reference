# Third-party references

The MIT license applies to this repository's original instructions, editorial analysis, and original exercises. It does not relicense quoted source material, trademarks, or content on linked websites.

The reference files identify the authors, titles, URLs, reading scope, and relevant publication or access dates of the works they discuss. Short quotations remain attributed to their respective sources. The repository does not include full books, articles, papers, video transcripts, images, or third-party Skill packages.

Some reference files discuss sources published under specific licenses, including Ruan Yifeng's blog (CC BY-NC-ND 3.0), WangDoc's JavaScript tutorial (CC BY-SA 3.0), Humanizer by Siqi Chen (MIT), and Stop Slop by Hardik Pandya (MIT). These are source annotations recorded in the Skill, rather than a claim that those works have been relicensed here. See the linked original works for their terms and versions. No upstream Skill rule set or tutorial source code is bundled.

Before reusing a quotation or an external work separately, check the original source and its applicable terms. References to an author, institution, product, or project do not imply affiliation or endorsement.

- Thomas S. Kane, *The Oxford Essential Guide to Writing*: selected passages from a supplied PDF, with the scanned Berkley 2000 edition information recorded.
